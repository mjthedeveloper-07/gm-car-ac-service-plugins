# GM Car AC Revenue OS Marketplace Brief

## Positioning

GM Car AC Revenue OS is a free-first AI revenue operating system for GM Car A/C Service. It helps the owner and team generate leads, improve conversion, create ad campaigns, handle WhatsApp and phone sales, improve landing pages, organize local SEO content, support fleet outreach, drive retention, and grow the 27-day INR 52,999 technician course.

## Revenue Target

Top-level target: INR 10 crore annual revenue.

Every agent connects output to at least one revenue path:

- Service bookings
- Higher average order value
- Care plan sales
- Fleet contracts
- General service upsells
- Training workshop enrollments
- Retargeting
- Referrals
- Retention

## Agents

- `gm-car-ac-growth-orchestrator`: routes all revenue workflows
- `gm-service-offer-builder`: builds offers
- `gm-landing-page-conversion-agent`: rewrites and tests landing pages
- `gm-ad-creative-agent`: creates ads and test plans
- `gm-whatsapp-sales-agent`: builds WhatsApp sales flows
- `gm-call-script-agent`: writes phone scripts
- `gm-local-seo-agent`: creates local SEO and GBP content
- `gm-fleet-outreach-agent`: creates B2B fleet outreach
- `gm-training-workshop-agent`: grows workshop enrollments
- `gm-social-media-agent`: creates organic content systems
- `gm-retention-agent`: drives reviews, referrals, care plans, reactivation
- `gm-analytics-agent`: creates tracking and weekly reports
- `gm-competitor-reach-agent`: analyzes Ambattur competitors and reach methods

## Free-First Tooling Plan

Core functionality requires no paid services. Recommended optional integrations:

- Local filesystem for plugin, skill, reference, and asset work
- Local git CLI for diffs, branches, commits, and releases
- Browser/fetch or Playwright for inspecting gmcaracservice.com and testing pages
- SQLite for a lightweight local lead/content/campaign database
- Google Sheets free account for lead trackers, calendars, and weekly reports
- Gmail free account for drafting outreach only with explicit approval
- GitHub free account for plugin source, issues, and releases
- Supabase free tier only if hosted storage is requested
- Vercel free tier only if hosted landing page previews are requested
- Notion free account only if content calendar or CRM workspace is requested

Do not send emails, publish posts, modify live websites, or message customers without explicit user approval.

## Reach Priority

1. Google Business Profile and local SEO for high-intent "near me" searches.
2. Instagram `@gmcaracservice` Reels and story replies for local awareness and proof.
3. WhatsApp-first intake, booking, missed-call recovery, and follow-up.
4. Google Search ads for urgent A/C intent.
5. Meta local awareness and retargeting for service, fleet, and workshop funnels.
6. Fleet outreach to taxis, delivery fleets, vans, school buses, used-car dealers, and local garages.
7. Review, referral, care-plan, and maintenance reminder systems.
