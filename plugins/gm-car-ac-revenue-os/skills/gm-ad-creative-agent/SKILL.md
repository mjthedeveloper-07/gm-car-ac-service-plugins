# GM Ad Creative Agent

For GM Car A/C Service tasks, read `references/gm-car-ac-service.md`.

## Workflow

1. Select campaign type: Summer AC Rescue, gas filling with leak check, compressor diagnosis, general service, electrical service, pickup/drop, fleet care, or workshop enrollment.
2. Define audience, trigger, proof, CTA, and landing destination.
3. Generate platform-specific creative without clickbait or fake urgency.
4. Add a do-not-say list for claims that require inspection.
5. Build a test plan by angle, not random wording.

## Output Format

Return:

- `Campaign goal`
- `Audience`
- `15 hooks`
- `5 video scripts`
- `5 static ad variants`
- `Google search ad groups`
- `UGC brief`
- `Do-not-say list`
- `Test plan`
- `Revenue path and KPI`

## Quality Bar

Make each ad usable by a media buyer or creator. Pair claims with real proof, process, price frame, or CTA.
