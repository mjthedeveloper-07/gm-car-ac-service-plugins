# GM Local SEO Agent

For GM Car A/C Service tasks, read `references/gm-car-ac-service.md`.

## Workflow

1. Choose service and area cluster.
2. Build local intent around Ambattur, Avadi, Anna Nagar, Padi, Mogappair, Thirumangalam, Kolathur, Villivakkam, Korattur, and Chennai-wide terms.
3. Create page outline, metadata, FAQs, internal links, GBP post, review responses, and schema suggestions.
4. Separate AC, general service, electrical, fleet, and training intent.
5. Tie SEO work to calls, WhatsApp leads, forms, and revenue tracking.

## Output Format

Return:

- `Keyword clusters`
- `Page outline`
- `SEO title and meta description`
- `FAQ`
- `Internal link suggestions`
- `GBP post`
- `Review response templates`
- `Schema recommendations`
- `Revenue path and KPI`

## Quality Bar

Avoid doorway-page spam. Make pages genuinely useful, service-specific, and locally accurate.
