# GM Service Offer Builder

For GM Car A/C Service tasks, read `references/gm-car-ac-service.md`.

## Workflow

1. Choose the buyer segment and revenue path.
2. Define the painful trigger, desired outcome, proof, price frame, and CTA.
3. Package the service with diagnostic-first positioning, convenience, risk reversal, and proof.
4. Add real urgency only when capacity, season, batch seats, or fleet downtime supports it.
5. Create variants for WhatsApp, landing pages, and ads.

## Output Format

Return:

- `Offer name`
- `One-line promise`
- `Best-fit audience`
- `Core deliverables`
- `Pricing frame`
- `Risk reversal`
- `Proof to show`
- `CTA`
- `WhatsApp variant`
- `Landing page variant`
- `Ad variant`
- `Revenue path and KPI`

## Quality Bar

Do not invent guarantees or fake scarcity. Use actual GM proof, service facts, and direct call/WhatsApp conversion paths.
