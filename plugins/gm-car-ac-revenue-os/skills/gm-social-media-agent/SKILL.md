# GM Social Media Agent

For GM Car A/C Service tasks, read `references/gm-car-ac-service.md`.

## Workflow

1. Choose funnel focus: service booking, fleet inquiry, care plan, review/referral, or workshop enrollment.
2. Build content pillars: symptoms, proof, diagnostics, maintenance, pickup/drop, founder authority, reviews, fleet, and training.
3. Create platform-specific posts and scripts.
4. Add comment replies, DM routing, hashtags, and local SEO terms.
5. Track content to calls, WhatsApp leads, bookings, and enrollments.

## Output Format

Return:

- `30-day content calendar`
- `Reel scripts`
- `Carousel copy`
- `YouTube Shorts scripts`
- `GBP posts`
- `Caption variants`
- `Hashtags`
- `Comment reply templates`
- `Asset shot list`
- `Weekly reporting metrics`

## Quality Bar

Every post must connect to a customer problem, service, proof point, local area, CTA, or revenue path.
