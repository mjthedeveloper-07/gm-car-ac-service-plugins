# Ad Hook Generator

## Core Workflow

1. Identify the audience, pain, offer, channel, proof, and conversion action.
2. Choose angles that map to buyer awareness: problem-aware, solution-aware, offer-aware, urgent trigger, proof, price, convenience, or risk reversal.
3. Write hooks that create relevance in the first 2 seconds without clickbait.
4. Generate variants for video, static, carousel, search, and UGC when relevant.
5. Add a test plan so winners can be compared by angle, not random wording.

For GM Car AC Service tasks, read `references/gm-car-ac-service.md`.

## Hook Angles

Use a mix of:

- Symptom: "AC not cooling even after gas filling?"
- Cost of delay: "A small leak can become a compressor bill."
- Price clarity: "Car AC gas filling from INR 1,500 with leak check."
- Proof: "6,000+ Chennai vehicles serviced."
- Convenience: "Free pickup and drop across Chennai."
- Seasonality: "Summer slots fill fast."
- Authority: founder, technician, process, tools, diagnostics.
- Myth busting: explain common mistakes or false assumptions.

## Output Format

Return:

- `Audience and campaign goal`
- `10 hooks`
- `5 short-form video scripts`: hook, scene, voiceover, on-screen text, CTA
- `5 static ad variants`: headline, body, CTA, visual direction
- `UGC brief`: creator persona, talking points, proof to show, do-not-say list
- `A/B test plan`: angle, hypothesis, success metric, next iteration

## Quality Bar

Make every hook usable by a media buyer or creator. Avoid vague hype. Pair each hook with a visual or proof element that can actually be produced.

# GM Car AC Service Reference

Use this when creating ads for gmcaracservice.com.

## Business Snapshot

- Business: GM Car A/C Service
- Location: Ambattur, Chennai
- Core service: car AC repair, AC gas filling, compressor repair, condenser replacement, cooling coil service, auto electrical service
- Proof: 7+ years experience, 6,000+ vehicles served, 4.9 Google rating, 500+ reviews
- CTA: call or WhatsApp +91 8428000085
- Convenience: free pickup and drop across Chennai
- Entry offer: AC gas filling from INR 1,500 single module, INR 2,200 double module, includes leak check
- Training offer: 3-day hands-on car AC service workshop for INR 899

## Creative Angles

- Chennai heat and urgent cooling problem
- "Gas filling did not last" leak-check education
- Same-day repair
- Free pickup/drop for busy owners
- Transparent price from INR 1,500
- 6,000+ vehicles and 4.9 rating
- Dealer-quality repair at non-dealer pricing
- Founder-led expert diagnosis
- Commercial vehicle and bus AC specialist
- Hands-on career training workshop

## Visual Ideas

- Thermometer or vent temperature before and after
- Technician checking AC pressure gauges
- Condenser/compressor diagnosis
- Customer handover
- Pickup/drop handoff
- Google review overlay
- Workshop footage with real vehicles
- Training students working on a live vehicle

## Do Not Say

- Do not promise all repairs in one hour.
- Do not claim a fixed price for compressor or major parts without model details.
- Do not say gas filling solves every cooling issue.
- Do not invent review quotes or warranty terms.
