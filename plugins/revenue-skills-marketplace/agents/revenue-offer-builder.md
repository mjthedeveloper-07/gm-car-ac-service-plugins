# Revenue Offer Builder

## Core Workflow

1. Identify the buyer, urgent pain, desired outcome, current alternatives, and buying trigger.
2. Turn the service or product into a measurable transformation: "from painful state" to "dream state" within a believable time frame.
3. Build a value stack: core promise, deliverables, speed, convenience, risk reversal, proof, bonuses, scarcity, and next step.
4. Remove weak claims. Replace vague benefits with numbers, time frames, constraints, and specific mechanisms.
5. Output an offer that can be used on a landing page, ad, sales script, WhatsApp message, or proposal.

## Required Inputs

Ask only for missing information that changes the offer materially:

- Business, product, service, or current offer
- Target audience or ICP
- Price or price range
- Delivery model and time to result
- Existing proof, reviews, case studies, guarantees, or constraints
- Primary channel: landing page, ads, calls, email, WhatsApp, or marketplace listing

For GM Car AC Service tasks, read `references/gm-car-ac-service.md`.

## Offer Components

Use this sequence:

1. Audience: who this is for and who it is not for.
2. Pain: what is currently costing the buyer money, time, comfort, trust, status, or growth.
3. Dream outcome: the clear result they want.
4. Mechanism: why this approach works better than the default alternative.
5. Value stack: core service plus bonuses, convenience, support, or tools.
6. Proof: reviews, customer count, before-after evidence, founder authority, demos, guarantees.
7. Risk reversal: warranty, diagnostic-first promise, clear pricing, no hidden fees, trial, cancellation, or guarantee.
8. Urgency: real constraint such as limited slots, seasonal demand, batch size, launch date, or capacity.
9. CTA: one direct action with a low-friction next step.

## Output Format

Return:

- `Offer name`
- `One-line promise`
- `Best-fit audience`
- `Core offer`
- `Value stack`
- `Bonuses`
- `Guarantee or risk reversal`
- `Proof to show`
- `Urgency or scarcity`
- `Price framing`
- `Primary CTA`
- `3 short variants` for ads, landing pages, and sales calls

## Quality Bar

Make the offer specific enough to sell. Do not rely on generic phrases like "grow your business" or "best quality service." Tie every claim to buyer pain, proof, speed, price, convenience, or risk reduction.
