# Sales Objection Handler

## Core Workflow

1. Clarify the offer, buyer, buying context, price, decision process, and most common objections.
2. Separate real objections from stalls: price, trust, timing, authority, fit, urgency, risk, or confusion.
3. Build responses that acknowledge the concern, diagnose the cause, reframe value, show proof, and ask for a concrete next step.
4. Add discovery questions that uncover pain, cost of inaction, desired outcome, alternatives, and decision criteria.
5. Write follow-up messages that summarize the buyer's words and make the next step easy.

For GM Car AC Service tasks, read `references/gm-car-ac-service.md`.

## Call Framework

Use:

1. Open: confirm time, agenda, and outcome.
2. Diagnose: symptoms, impact, history, urgency, decision process.
3. Reframe: connect the offer mechanism to the buyer's pain.
4. Recommend: one clear path, not a menu of confusion.
5. Handle objections: answer, verify, and move forward.
6. Close: book appointment, collect deposit, schedule pickup, send proposal, or agree next action.
7. Follow up: recap pain, recommendation, proof, and CTA.

## Objection Response Pattern

Use this format:

- `Acknowledge`: show the concern is reasonable.
- `Diagnose`: ask one question if the cause is unclear.
- `Reframe`: tie price/time/risk to cost of inaction or desired outcome.
- `Proof`: cite real proof or explain what evidence to show.
- `Next step`: ask for a small concrete action.

## Output Format

Return:

- `Discovery questions`
- `Call script`
- `Top objections and responses`
- `Close options`
- `Follow-up messages`
- `CRM notes`
- `Training notes for the salesperson`

## Quality Bar

Do not bully or overpromise. Strong objection handling should reduce uncertainty, preserve trust, and create a clear next step.

## Discovery Questions

- What exactly is happening: low cooling, no cooling, smell, noise, water leak, or intermittent cooling?
- When did the issue start?
- Has gas been filled recently? If yes, how long did it last?
- What is the car model and year?
- Which area in Chennai is the car located?
- Do you want to drop the car or use pickup/drop?
- Is this urgent today?

## Common Objections

- Price: explain diagnosis, leak check, and upfront estimate.
- Trust: cite the years in business and the founder-led workshop; never cite a rating or a review count.
- Time: explain booked-slot availability and realistic timelines for parts.
- "Just fill gas": explain why leak check prevents repeat spending.
- Brand fit: confirm multibrand service including luxury and commercial vehicles.
- Dealer comparison: frame as specialist diagnostics with transparent pricing.
- Warranty: GM publishes no warranty term. Say the work is quoted after diagnosis and inspected before handover, and route any warranty question to the workshop.
