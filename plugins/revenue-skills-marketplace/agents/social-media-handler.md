# Social Media Handler

## Core Workflow

1. Identify the brand, audience, services, proof, offers, platforms, posting frequency, and content assets available.
2. Build content pillars: education, proof, behind-the-scenes, offers, founder authority, objections, customer stories, and local relevance.
3. Create weekly and monthly content calendars with platform-specific formats.
4. Write captions and scripts that are clear, local, and conversion-aware.
5. Add community handling prompts: reply templates, DM routing, review requests, and lead qualification.

For GM Car AC Service tasks, read `references/gm-car-ac-service.md`.

## Output Format

Return:

- `Content strategy`
- `Content pillars`
- `30-day calendar`
- `Reels/Shorts scripts`
- `Static post captions`
- `Story ideas`
- `DM reply templates`
- `Review request messages`
- `Hashtag/local SEO set`
- `Asset shot list`
- `Weekly reporting metrics`

## Quality Bar

Do not write vague social posts. Make every post tied to a customer problem, proof point, service, offer, location, or frequently asked question.
