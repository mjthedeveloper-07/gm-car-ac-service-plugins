# Landing Page Rewriter

## Core Workflow

1. Identify the traffic source, audience intent, offer, and conversion goal.
2. Audit the current page for message clarity, trust proof, friction, objections, and CTA strength.
3. Rewrite from top to bottom using the buyer's decision sequence: relevance, pain, outcome, mechanism, proof, offer, risk reversal, CTA.
4. Keep the page scannable. Prefer short sections, concrete proof, and one dominant action.
5. Preserve facts from the source. Flag unsupported claims instead of inventing proof.

For GM Car AC Service tasks, read `references/gm-car-ac-service.md`.

## Page Structure

Use this structure unless the user asks for a different format:

1. Hero: audience, urgent pain, dream outcome, mechanism, CTA, trust proof.
2. Problem: symptoms, hidden cost, why the default fix fails.
3. Offer: service, deliverables, timeline, price frame, convenience.
4. Proof: reviews, customer count, photos, before-after, founder credibility.
5. Process: simple steps from inquiry to result.
6. Objections: price, trust, time, risk, location, complexity, alternatives.
7. FAQ: answer search and buying questions directly.
8. Final CTA: repeat the action with urgency and reassurance.

## Diagnostic Checklist

Check:

- Is the first screen clear within 5 seconds?
- Is the niche explicit?
- Is the CTA specific and repeated?
- Is proof near the claim it supports?
- Are price, timeline, and next step clear?
- Does the page answer "why now"?
- Does the lead form ask only what the business needs to respond?
- Are local SEO terms included naturally when relevant?

## Output Format

Return:

- `Conversion diagnosis`: 5 to 8 specific issues
- `Rewritten hero`: headline, subheadline, proof strip, CTA
- `Section-by-section rewrite`
- `FAQ objections`
- `CTA variants`
- `Testing notes`: headline, offer, CTA, proof, form, or pricing tests

## Quality Bar

Do not create a generic marketing page. Tie copy to the buyer's immediate job-to-be-done, the business's real proof, and the channel bringing the visitor.
